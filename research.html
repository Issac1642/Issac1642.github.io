Kinetic structure of near-Sun shock waves

Current

PhD, University of Turku · since January 2026

My doctoral work characterizes the kinetic structure of shocks close to the Sun from observations, combining Parker Solar Probe and Solar Orbiter data. It is supported by a doctoral research grant on interplanetary shock waves from the Space Research Laboratory (2026).

  • Parker Solar Probe
  • Solar Orbiter
  • Shock waves

Large- and small-scale solar magnetic fields and X-ray flux

MS thesis · May 2024 to April 2025 · Dr. Gopal Hazra, IIT Kanpur

I related the Sun's large- and small-scale photospheric magnetic fields to its soft X-ray flux. I separated the scales with a spherical harmonic decomposition (lmax = 5, 10, 20) and an adaptive threshold on SHARP active regions, then computed Pearson correlations with the X-ray flux.

  • HMI
  • MDI
  • GOES/XRS
  • TIMED/SEE
  • SORCE/XPS

Penumbral spots in the RGO Sunspots Database

December 2023 to February 2024 · Dr. Theodosios Chatzistergos, Max Planck Institute for Solar System Research

I wrote code that extracts penumbral spots from the database and compares spots with no umbra to spots with umbra: area and number of groups over time and per cycle, 1875 to 2013.

  • RGO database
  • Sunspots

Small-scale transients in the solar atmosphere

May to July 2023 · Dr. Girjesh R. Gupta, Udaipur Solar Observatory, PRL

I fitted a power law to the frequency distribution of flare peaks in Chandrayaan-2 XSM time series and did a case study of the 5-minute oscillation in GONG data.

  • Chandrayaan-2 XSM
  • GONG
  • Power law
